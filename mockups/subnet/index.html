Subnet page — three directions

Three different ways to visualize what miners and validators do on a subnet. All three use SN18 Zeus (weather forecasting) as the example. All three include the same one-line pitch at the top.

What's the same across all three: one-sentence pitch at top, then the miner ↔ validator diagram, then a short role table. Differences are entirely in the diagram metaphor.

How to read: open all three side-by-side in different tabs and compare. The one that makes you say "yes I'd send this to someone who has 30 seconds" is the winner. Tell me which (or call out the parts you like from each).

Generalization plan: whichever wins becomes the template at src/pages/beginner/subnets/[slug].astro. The template reads the same data shape (input, output, verifier, hardware) for every subnet — so it works for Chutes, Masa, Templar, Affine, etc. without per-subnet hand-crafting.
