Zeus

Zeus is a subnet that forecasts the weather — miners run global atmospheric models, validators score their predictions against ground-truth ERA5 reanalysis, and the customer outside the network is the energy-trading desk.

Read once and you know what's being made (a global forecast), how it's judged (anomaly-correlation-coefficient against the held-out ground truth), and who's paying for it in the real world.

One tempo · what happens between miner and validator

1 tempo ≈ 72 min · ~20 cycles per day
Step 01 · Challenge
Validator

Sample a problem

Pick a region, pick a recent time slice the miners haven't seen, broadcast the initial atmospheric state to every active miner.

payload ERA5 init tensor · ~4 MB
Step 02 · Compute
Miner

Run the model

Each miner runs its own forecast model and returns gridded predictions at +6h, +24h, +72h, +120h horizons.

latency 20–90 s on H100
Step 03 · Score
Validator

Grade with ACC

Compute anomaly-correlation-coefficient against the held-out ERA5 truth window. Weighted sum across variables and horizons.

scale 0.0 → 1.0 · higher = better
Step 04 · Settle
Subtensor

Yuma → emission

Every validator's weights, multiplied by their stake, aggregated by Yuma into one reward vector. TAO mints to top miners.

tempo ~72 min · 24× daily
A single tempo, end to end
t=0 · validator broadcasts
t=2m · miners compute
t=3m · validators score
t=72m · chain settles

Who does what

MINER

Produces a forecast.

doesRuns an atmospheric model and returns gridded predictions every tempo.
hardware1×H100 80GB (recommended) · 1×A100 minimum
paid forHigh ACC against the held-out truth. Heavy-tailed: top 10% earn 5–10× median.
paid viaPer-tempo emission, proportional to validator score × validator stake.
VALIDATOR

Grades against truth.

doesPulls the held-out ERA5 window, runs ACC against every miner's response, submits weights.
requiresTop-N stake position + an ERA5 mirror + reference validator code.
paid forSubmitting weights that agree with the consensus median across other validators.
paid viaPer-tempo emission, proportional to (stake × consensus alignment).
